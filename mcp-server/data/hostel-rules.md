# Nimbus University – Hostel Rules and Residential Code of Conduct

**Document Ref:** NU/DSA/HOSTEL/2026-V1  
**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026  
**Governing Authority:** Office of the Dean of Student Affairs

---

## 1. General Principles
Residence in Nimbus University hostels is a privilege extended to registered students. Residents must conduct themselves with maturity, dignity, and mutual respect.

## 2. Curfew and Biometric Timings
- **Main Hostel Gate Closure**: 22:00 (10:00 PM) sharp on all days.
- **Biometric Attendance**: Mandatory attendance check-in between 21:00 and 22:00 every evening.
- **Late Entries**: Any arrival between 22:00 and 22:30 requires signature in the Late Entry Register and prior warden intimation. After 22:30, entry requires express permission from the Chief Warden.

## 3. Visitor Policy
- **Visiting Hours**: 16:00 to 19:30 on weekdays; 10:00 to 19:30 on weekends and official holidays.
- **Visitor Locations**: Visitors are strictly restricted to the Common Recreation Room and Ground Floor Lobby. Under no circumstances may visitors enter private resident rooms.
- **Overnight Guests**: Overnight stays by non-residents or day-scholars are strictly prohibited and attract a disciplinary fine of INR 5,000.

## 4. Electrical Appliances and Safety
- Authorized appliances: Laptop chargers, mobile chargers, personal study lamps (LED).
- Strictly prohibited appliances: Electric kettles, induction stoves, immersion heaters, room heaters, and air conditioners. Unauthorized heating equipment will be confiscated immediately.
- Tampering with electrical wiring or circuit breakers will result in disciplinary suspension.

## 5. Substance Policy and Discipline
- The entire university campus, including hostel premises, is a strictly designated alcohol-free, smoke-free, and substance-free zone.
- Possession, distribution, or consumption of narcotics, alcohol, or tobacco constitutes gross misconduct, leading to immediate expulsion from the hostel and referral to the University Disciplinary Committee.

## 6. Quiet Hours and Noise
- **Quiet Hours**: 23:00 to 06:00. High-volume music, disruptive gatherings, and noisy activities are prohibited during quiet hours to protect the study environment.
